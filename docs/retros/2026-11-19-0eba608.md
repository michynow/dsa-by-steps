# Retro: Learn/Practice redesign (`ddc2ee5..0eba608`)

This was the third `/software-factory` run. It built the content contract v2 (WP-12), Learn/Practice modes (WP-13), term chips with Key Idea, story and uses (WP-14), the "Your turn" check card (WP-15), the play-queue Linked lists (WP-16) and step playback (WP-17). Every package needed at most two review rounds. Two usage-limit interruptions cost one restart each.

## Findings

| #   | Finding                                                                                                                                                  | Gap      | Why it got through                                                                     |
