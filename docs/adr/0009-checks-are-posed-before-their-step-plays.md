# Checks are posed before their Step plays

A Step that carries a Check first shows the Scene as it was before the Step's Step Events, with the Check's prompt in the Caption Strip. Only once the Check is settled (passed or revealed) do the Step's events animate and the Caption and explanation appear. A Check shown on the Scene after its Step would have its answer drawn on screen, so Predict Checks would test reading, not reasoning.

## Consequences

- Predict and Order targets are validated against the Scene before the Step, not after it.
- A Step's Caption is shown with the change, so it may explain the answer; the Check prompt alone carries the question.
